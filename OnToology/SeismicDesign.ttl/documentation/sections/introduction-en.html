Introduction back to ToC

Fragility categories in FEMA P-58 depend on the seismic design category. For example, the D2021 piping categories separate SDC A or B, SDC C, SDC D/E/F, and SDC D/E/F under OSHPD. IFC models do not normally carry this value, so it has to be supplied from outside the BIM data. The Seismic Design ontology gives that value a formal home.
