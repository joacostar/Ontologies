Description

Building Components

bc:

https://w3id.org/utoronto/ontologies/BuildingComponents#

To represent building components, reusing existing classes representing building components from the Building Product Ontology (prod:). It also imports a subset of the Ontology of units of measure (om2:).

 

Seismic Design

sd:

https://w3id.org/utoronto/ontologies/SeismicDesign#

Seismic design category values and property to attach these to building components. It imports the Building Components ontology(bc:).

SeisFront

sfront:

https://w3id.org/utoronto/ontologies/SeisFront#

Fragility categories are represented as equivalent OWL classes. It imports the following ontologies:

·        Ontology of units of measure (om2:)

·        Digital Construction Building Material Ontology (dicm:)

·        Building Components (bc:)

·        Seismic Design (sd:)

 

 

Lite Terminology ifcOWL

ifcLit:

https://w3id.org/utoronto/ontologies/IfcLit#

Complement to ifcOWL with composite OPs and DPs which directly connect subject with final object. It imports ifcOWL for IFC4 (ifc:) and the Digital Construction Building Material Ontology (dicm:).

 

SeisFront Alignment

sfa:

https://w3id.org/utoronto/ontologies/SeisFrontAlignment#

 

Provides the alignment between ifcOWL + ifcLit and Building Components and SeisFront.

Building Product Ontology

(Pauwels, 2020)

prod:

https://w3id.org/product#

To represent structural, nonstructural, and system components of a building.

Ontology of units of measure

(Rijgersberg et al., 2013)

om2:

http://www.ontology-of-units-of-measure.org/resource/om-2/subset#

To represent physical units.

Digital Construction Building Material Ontology

(Valluru et al., 2020)

dicm:

https://w3id.org/digitalconstruction/0.5/Materials/Subset#

To model building construction materials.

ifcOWL

(Pauwels and Terkaj, 2016)

ifc:

https://standards.buildingsmart.org/IFC/DEV/IFC4/ADD2_TC1/OWL#

Ontology of the IFC4 schema.
